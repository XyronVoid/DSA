# Space Bachao – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Space Bachao ("the app") compresses photos, videos and PDF files on your Android phone to free up storage space.

## The short version
- **Your files never leave your phone.** The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot upload or send anything.
- We do **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app accesses and why
| Access | Why |
|---|---|
| Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO) | To find large photos and videos and compress them. This is the core function of the app. |
| Photo/video location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) | So that location information inside your photos and videos is kept after compression and is not lost. |
| Permission to modify files (Android system popup) | To replace a file with its smaller version, only after you confirm. |
| PDF files you pick | Only the PDFs you choose yourself, to compress them. |

All processing happens on your device. Original files that are replaced are kept in the app's private "Bin" on your phone for the period you choose (or not at all if you turn the Bin off), so you can restore them. You can empty the Bin at any time.

## Payments
"Space Bachao Pro" is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by **Google Play**. The app never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data deletion
The app stores only its own settings and the Bin on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
